On a Hoh Rainforest campsite, our camper neighbor across the road was a majestic mountain man, who had brought his whole family out camping together, in his big house tent. He was washing his breakfast dishes in a big soapy plastic tub, and I told him how I noticed he had his whole family in the tent there, his dog, wife, and little baby son and daughter. I thought that was fun and wonderful. He told me it was his little kids' first camping.

"I'm getting them dialed," he told me with big teeth.

What a blessing to be Dialed! I'd like to be dialed. Maybe that's what Thoreau was [trying to do at Walden](https://www.goodreads.com/quotes/2690-i-went-to-the-woods-because-i-wished-to-live). Get dialed on not living "what was not life." The opposite of getting dialed is probably social media.
