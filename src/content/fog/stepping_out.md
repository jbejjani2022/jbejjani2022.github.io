All those layers
you and I wove around you
maybe you can step out of them
before we forget what we wanted so badly to protect


*With appreciation for the fourth of Sarah Downey's ``[Hard-earned truths](https://www.sarahadowney.com/post/hard-earned-truths-i-ve-learned-about-the-universe)''*
